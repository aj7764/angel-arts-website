About Angel Arts

Teaching practical bakery skills with heart, hands, and real-world experience.

Our Story

Angel Arts & Culinary Academy was born from a simple passion: making great baking accessible to everyone. Founded in Trichy, we believe that baking is both an art and a practical skill that can transform lives — whether you're baking for your family, starting a home business, or pursuing a professional career.

Our founder, with years of experience in both traditional and modern baking techniques, saw a gap in hands-on, practical training. Too many courses focus on theory; we focus on getting your hands in the dough, learning by doing, and building confidence through practice.

Today, we're proud to have trained hundreds of students who've gone on to start their own bakeries, work in professional kitchens, or simply bring joy to their families through beautiful, delicious baked goods.

Our Training Kitchen

Our training kitchen gives you live practice — not just demos.

  • ✅ Fully equipped professional-grade kitchen
  • ✅ Individual workstations for hands-on learning
  • ✅ Commercial ovens, mixers, and tools
  • ✅ Small batch sizes (max 8–10 students per class)
  • ✅ All ingredients and materials provided
  • ✅ Take home everything you bake

We believe the best way to learn baking is by doing. That's why every class is hands-on, and every student leaves with real experience and delicious results.
